Viridian Fields

A matte botanical console. Deep emerald is the action, an engraved fern is the material, and nothing on the page emits light. This is the one place in the Li//OS desk family that is absorbed rather than lit: the accent is a dark green fill, the ink on it is the lightest thing in the kit, and the only glow is the place's own 14px emerald at 26%, on the primary button.

Overview

Every other dark kit in this library answers "how does it glow?" This one answers "what does a surface look like when it has stopped reflecting?" Four decisions carry it:

  • The accent is dark, and it inverts the ink. {colors.primary} is the place's own --acc, a deep emerald that is darker than the text around it. So ink on it is light ({colors.on-primary}) and the accent used as a label is its pale member {colors.tertiary} — the fill itself is 3.4:1 on a pane and cannot be a small label. Five of the six places in this family do the opposite; here it is deliberate and the numbers are in the README.
  • The material is a plate, not a gradient. {colors.surface} is the desk's own .card-in: a sheen band with a 1px specular lip, a bottom occlusion band, the engraved fern, an emerald identity edge along the floor, a 5% tint, a 157° face fall, and the face. The fern is drawn once at 248px and pinned to the bottom-right — it is an engraving on a plate, not a pattern.
  • The frame is 1px and nothing behind it moves. The page ground is two soft fields of {colors.bg-stop-1} over a 120px technical ruling, one 520px fern struck near the floor, and a ground-to-floor ramp. Large-celled and quiet, so the card's plate is legible as a plate.
  • Depth is occlusion. --shadow-1 and --shadow-2 are black drops; --blur: none because every surface is opaque. Nothing in this kit is glass, and a blurred opaque card only costs a repaint.

Colors

{colors.primary} is the desk's --acc (#147A57): a matte, absorbed emerald with 0.15 relative luminance. It fills the primary button, the progress ramp and the avatar — the only opaque fills in the kit. {colors.tertiary} (#5BCBA0, the desk's --accHi) is the same hue re-tuned for text: eyebrows, links, active nav, secondary-button labels, badge-accent. It measures 9.0:1 on the flat pane, 6.4:1 on a card's lit end and 5.9:1 over the brightest point of the scene field.

{colors.secondary} (#7FA9C8) is the place's own pale sky — the second hue, for gradients, media, charts and the avatar's far stop. It is a fill colour and is never a label.

The status ladder is derived from the place, and the pair that needed care is {colors.ok} against the accent: both are green, so {colors.ok} is held clearly lighter and mintier — 0.50 relative luminance against the accent's 0.15, 2.8:1 apart — and a state chip is separated from the action by material as well: a badge is an outlined chip, the action is an opaque plate.

{colors.neutral} is the floor the whole kit was graded against, and {colors.bg-stop-1} is the lightest field on the page — the scene's own --sceneA. {colors.fern} documents the plate's stroke colour (white, at the 9% the desk baked into its alpha); it is a drawn asset, not a tint.

Typography

Chakra Petch for display and body, JetBrains Mono for labels, keys and metadata — the family's fixed pairing, shared by all six places, because the type is the OS. Display weights stay at 500–600: this register is printed on a plate rather than shouted off one, and a 700 weight at display size is the quickest way to make a matte kit read as a loud one.

Labels are uppercase mono at {typography.label} (12px floor) with {typography.label.letterSpacing} — the desk's instrument voice, narrow enough to sit inside a 3px chip radius.

Layout

The page is a plate laid on a field: generous vertical rhythm ({spacing.lg} between sections, {spacing.xl} for hero pacing), 1px rules, and a card radius of 5px so a plate reads as cut rather than moulded. Nothing overlaps; the kit's depth comes from the drop under a plate, not from stacking translucent layers.

The signature plate is anchored to the card's bottom-right corner (the desk's own right -42px bottom 10%), so a card's engraving always reads as a mark struck into that corner rather than as a centred illustration. On the ground the same engraving appears once at 520px near the floor of the page — one plate, at roughly half the card's alpha. It does not repeat.

Elevation & Depth

Two shadows, both black, both short: 0 6px 16px rgba(0,0,0,.50) for a resting plate and 0 10px 26px rgba(0,0,0,.55) for one that floats. The single emanation is 0 0 14px rgba(46,156,116,.26) — the place's own glow, at the desk's own width, on the primary button. Widening that blur, or lifting its alpha, is what would turn this kit into Mainframe Access.

Shapes

{rounded.sm} 3px, {rounded.md} 5px, {rounded.lg} 7px, {rounded.pill} 999px. --cut: 0px and no --clip: this place rounds, it does not chamfer. Buttons take the 5px radius, chips and small controls 3px, and the native checkbox is squared to 3px with --check-radius so the shape language holds on controls the browser draws itself.

Components

  • Buttons. The primary is an opaque emerald plate with a light label and the faint emerald glow; its hover goes deeper (#116B4C), because raising the fill's luminance drops the light label toward 3.9:1. Secondary and danger buttons are transparent with an accent-hairline rim and their colour in the label. The press travels down (--btn-press: 0px against a −1px hover) so the key seats into the plate — the only motion in the kit, linear and .18s.
  • Cards. {colors.surface} is the plate material; card-elevated drops to {colors.surface-2} — the nested tier, which drops the engraving and the identity edge and keeps a shallow sheen plus half the tint.
  • Inputs. A shallow recess with a lit lower lip (--input-inset), a rim of {colors.border-strong} — the desk's accent hairline at 60% — and {colors.surface-2} as its field.
  • Badges. Pills at {rounded.pill} with a mono label; badge-accent sits on a 22% emerald tint under the pale ink. Status badges keep the chip's own surface and take only their dot and border from the status hue.
  • Navigation. A milled channel (--bar-shadow) with the current item on an emerald tint, in {colors.tertiary}.

Do's and Don'ts

  • Do treat the emerald as a fill and its pale member as the label. Writing --accent as text is the mistake this kit's ink pair exists to prevent.
  • Do keep the glow at 14px / 26%. It is the place's own value and its restraint is the identity.
  • Do let the fern sit in the bottom-right of a plate and leave it alone. It is drawn at 248px with its alpha baked in; re-tinting or tiling it makes it a pattern.
  • Don't lighten the ground to add contrast. The scene field is the lightest ground the kit was graded against, and every number in the README assumes it.
  • Don't use {colors.secondary} for text, and don't introduce a third "look at me" hue — the status ladder is the only other colour family the kit carries.
  • Don't add glass, backdrop blur or a coloured rim glow. All three are the opposite register.
